To leave behind more than a brokerage statement.

I wanted a place to document the thinking behind ownership: what I bought, what I misunderstood, what changed, and what still felt true after the market moved around.

Felix/August is meant to become a durable record of conviction, mistakes, patience, and discipline. I want the boys to be able to see not just the outcome of decisions but the reasoning behind them: why a business mattered, why a position was sized a certain way, what risks were visible, and what I hoped time would do for small repeated contributions.

What this is not

  • Not day-trading commentary.
  • Not a promise that every thesis will be right.
  • Not a substitute for caution around options or risk.
  • Not advice. The index fund is easy, and for most people it is the whole answer.

How it is kept

The numbers are never typed. Positions come from the brokerage exports through tools/import_positions.py, which also records the export date you see under the ledger. The Treasury curve and the quotes arrive on weekday evenings through a scheduled job. The reading list is a CSV. Contributions are the one list kept by hand, because no export carries them. The whole site is plain HTML, one stylesheet and one script, and the source is public.

Who writes it

Jason Fetzer, their father. I write software and optimization models for corrugated box plants, and I keep a separate site for that work at justfetz.github.io. The tools here are the finance side of the same habit: build the model yourself at least once, so you know which number it depends on.
