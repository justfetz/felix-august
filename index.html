Felix/August · a journal and a set of tools

The index fund is easy. If you want to go deeper, the tools are here.

This is the record of what our household owns, why, and what I was reading and building while it was built. Most people should buy the index and get on with their lives. But the quant firms average something like twenty percent a year, and it is worth some weeks of reading, building, and failing to understand how. Not always worth changing what you do. Always worth understanding what is going on.

Written for Felix and August. Every number below comes from the brokerage exports; nothing is typed in by hand except what we put in.

Tools

Models you can change the inputs on

The point of the tools is not the answer. It is seeing which assumption the answer depends on. Each one is built from code you can read, with every number computed, never typed.

I did not know every company that would matter, but I felt the direction clearly enough to begin. The notes on this site are meant to preserve that feeling: the moment when AI stopped looking like a curiosity and started looking like infrastructure.
