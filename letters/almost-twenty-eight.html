I didn't know what an index fund was until I was almost twenty-eight

I didn't know what an index fund really was until I was almost twenty-eight years old.

My mom was never great with money. I just knew, growing up, that so much of life depended on it. I watch her now in retirement, and the thought I can't shake is a simple one: there is no way I want the two of you to struggle in retirement.

At the same time, I am watching AI change the world. I don't know every company that will matter, but I can see the direction, and this time I understand enough to take part instead of watching.

So I want to do two things, and I want you to be able to watch me do them. Invest: small amounts, every week, in things I have taken the time to understand. And learn: read the books, build the tools, write down what I got wrong. The index fund is easy, and if that is all you ever do, you will be far ahead of where I was at twenty-eight. The rest of this site is for when you want to go deeper.

Start earlier than I did. That is most of what I have to say.

Dad
