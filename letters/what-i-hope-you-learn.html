What I hope you learn from this

Not a list of rules. More like the things I keep coming back to, written down so I can be held to them.

  • Small amounts matter when they are paired with time. Consistency beats intensity when time is on your side.
  • Ownership can be a way of learning, not just earning. It matters more than prediction.
  • Reading deeply is more valuable than reacting quickly.
  • Data should support judgment, not replace it.
  • Risk belongs in the open, especially with options.
  • Build the model yourself at least once, so you know which number it depends on.
  • Technology, infrastructure, and energy are deeply connected. Technology changes the world, but character still matters most.
  • Whatever this becomes, it is built by stewardship, not just returns.
